# Athar

Athar (أثر) is a platform for discovering, reading, saving and sharing authentic Hadiths. This
glossary fixes the words the product, the code and the squad use for its content and its readers.

## Content

**Hadith**:
A single authentic narration as published by a source collection. Never fictional, never composed
by Athar.
_Avoid_: quote, saying, verse

**Collection**:
A Hadith compilation offered by the provider, such as Sahih al-Bukhari.
_Avoid_: book (a book is a part of a collection), edition (a provider's per-language file), source

**Book**:
A division within a collection, as the collection itself numbers and names it.
_Avoid_: section (the provider's word; map it to book or chapter explicitly), volume

**Chapter**:
A division within a book, as the collection defines it.
_Avoid_: section, bab (in English copy)

**Reference**:
The exact location of a Hadith within its collection: Hadith number, and book and chapter where
known.
_Avoid_: citation, ID

**Arabic text**:
The original Arabic wording of a Hadith as the source provides it.
_Avoid_: original translation, Arabic translation

**Translation**:
A rendering of a Hadith into another language, always labelled with its language and kept
distinct from the Arabic text.
_Avoid_: text (unqualified), meaning, interpretation

**Narrator**:
The person a narration is attributed to by the source.
_Avoid_: author, speaker

**Authenticity grade**:
A source's classification of a Hadith's authenticity, always shown with who graded it. Shown only
when verified.
_Avoid_: rating, score, status

**Source attribution**:
The statement of where a Hadith and its metadata came from (collection, provider, grader).
_Avoid_: credit, link

**Topic**:
A theme a Hadith relates to, such as Salah or Sabr. Either source metadata or an explicitly
documented editorial categorization, and labelled as which.
_Avoid_: tag, category (unqualified)

**Editorial categorization**:
A topic assignment made by Athar's curators rather than by the source collection.
_Avoid_: classification (when it implies scholarly origin)

**Hadith of the Day**:
The one Hadith featured for a given calendar day, the same for every refresh of that day.
_Avoid_: daily random, featured hadith

**Hadith provider**:
An external data source of Hadiths, seen by Athar only through its normalized model.
_Avoid_: API (unqualified), backend

## Readers

**Visitor**:
Someone using Athar without an account; has every public discovery feature.
_Avoid_: guest user, anonymous user

**Reader**:
A person with an Athar account.
_Avoid_: member, customer, subscriber

**Bookmark**:
A Hadith a reader or visitor has saved.
_Avoid_: favourite, like, save (as a noun)

**Personal collection**:
A named list of Hadiths a reader creates, renames and fills. Never shortened to "collection".
_Avoid_: playlist, folder, list

**Reading history**:
The Hadiths a reader has viewed, with when they last read them.
_Avoid_: log, activity feed

**Reading activity**:
The days on which a reader read at least one Hadith.
_Avoid_: engagement, usage

**Reading streak**:
The count of consecutive days of reading activity up to today, shown subtly and optionally.
_Avoid_: score, points, level

**Share card**:
A downloadable image of a Hadith in one of Athar's templates, always carrying its reference and
Athar branding.
_Avoid_: poster, graphic, quote image
